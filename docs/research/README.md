## The one thing that cannot wait

Consent cannot be applied retroactively. The cohort already lined up to take
Genius Mining becomes research data only if they consent to research **before or
at the time they take it**. Everything else in these documents can be revised
later. That cannot.

See [`IRB_PROTOCOL.md`](./IRB_PROTOCOL.md), "Consent is the deadline".

## Provenance

The instrument implements the Genius Mining Starter, Tool 7 of *The Business of
Life: Student Edition* (Adam Bond Devereau, Hidden Genius Labs LLC, 2026). That
matters for a review board: this is a published, dated protocol with a documented
theoretical basis, not a set of questions assembled for the study. The mapping
from book to instrument is in
[`PILOT_STUDY_DESIGN.md`](./PILOT_STUDY_DESIGN.md#instrument-provenance).
